## 4. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

### 4.1 What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, as well as to provide information to the site owners.

### 4.2 How We Use Cookies

We use cookies for the following purposes:

- **Essential Cookies:** Required for the Site to function properly.
- **Analytics Cookies:** Help us understand how visitors interact with our Site.
- **Advertising Cookies:** Used to deliver relevant advertisements and measure ad campaign effectiveness.

### 4.3 Managing Cookies

Most browsers allow you to refuse or delete cookies. You can configure your browser to refuse cookies or to alert you when cookies are being sent. Please note that disabling cookies may affect the functionality of some features on our Site.

For more information about how to manage cookies, please visit [www.allaboutcookies.org](https://www.allaboutcookies.org).
