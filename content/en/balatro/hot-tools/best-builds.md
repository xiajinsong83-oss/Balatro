Proven winning build compositions for Balatro. These are the most consistent strategies that work across stake levels.

### 1. Mult Scaling Build (S-Tier)

**Core Jokers:** Fibonacci + Blueprint + Brainstorm + Campfire

**Strategy:** Focus entirely on Mult. Fibonacci gives +8 Mult per Ace/2/3/5/8 played. Blueprint copies the joker to its left. Brainstorm copies ALL jokers to its left. Stack these three for exponential Mult growth.

**Best Deck:** Red Deck (extra hand) or Blue Deck (extra discard)

### 2. Chip Stacking Build (A-Tier)

**Core Jokers:** Odd Todd + Even Steven + Pareidolia + Baron

**Strategy:** Maximize chip value. Odd Todd gives +31 chips per odd card. Even Steven gives +4 Mult per even card. Pareidolia makes all cards face cards, doubling their value.

**Best Deck:** Green Deck (-1 discard but +5 hand size)

### 3. Retrigger Build (A-Tier)

**Core Jokers:** Zany Joker + Yorick + Ride the Bus + Oops!

**Strategy:** Make cards score multiple times. Zany retriggers your first played card. Yorick retriggers spades. Stack retriggers on high-value cards (Aces, Polychrome) for massive score multipliers.

**Best Deck:** Yellow Deck (free retrigger voucher)

### 4. Economy Build (B-Tier)

**Core Jokers:** Golden Joker + Ride the Bus + Wealthy Joker + Trader

**Strategy:** Never run out of money. Golden Joker gives $2 every round. Buy all shop items, upgrade every card. This build is slow but extremely safe for new players.

**Best Deck:** Purple Deck (less starting money but +1 hand)

### Tips for Any Build

- **Save before risky picks:** If a legendary joker appears, save first. Some are traps.
- **Focus on one scaling type:** Don't mix chip and Mult builds early. Pick one and commit.
- **Planet cards matter:** Upgrading your poker hands' base Chips and Mult is more important than you think.
- **Buy Vouchers:** Telescope and Grabber are the best early Vouchers.
