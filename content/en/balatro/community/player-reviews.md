### What Players Love

- **Endless replayability** — No two runs feel the same
- **Joker synergy depth** — Discovering new combinations after 100+ hours
- **Perfect pacing** — Short runs that easily turn into long sessions
- **Outstanding soundtrack** — Casino jazz + electronic fusion

### Common Complaints

- **RNG can be brutal** — Some runs feel unwinnable from the first shop
- **Crash bugs** — Most fixed in recent patches but still reported on mobile
- **Steep learning curve** — The tutorial is thin, expect to lose your first 10 runs
