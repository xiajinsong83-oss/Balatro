### Our Mission

BalatroHub is built by players, for players. We created this site because we love Balatro and wanted a fast, clean, no-fluff place to look up jokers, calculate scores, check for bugs, and find answers to our questions mid-run.

### What We Offer

- **Interactive Tools:** Score calculator, joker database, and more on the way.
- **Daily Updates:** Patch notes, known bugs, and community FAQ refreshed automatically every day.
- **Clean Design:** Built for speed. Works great on desktop and mobile.
- **Community Driven:** Bug reports and FAQs are aggregated from real player discussions.

### Future Plans

We're planning to expand to more roguelike and strategy games. If there's a game you'd like to see covered, [contact us](/legal/contact-us/).
