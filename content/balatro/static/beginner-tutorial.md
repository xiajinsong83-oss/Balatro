New to Balatro? This guide covers the core mechanics you need to win your first run.

### The Goal

Beat 8 Antes (sets of 3 Blinds each) by playing poker hands that score above the Blind's target. You have limited hands and discards per round. After each Blind, visit the shop to buy jokers, tarot cards, and upgrades.

### Scoring Basics

Every poker hand gives base Chips and Mult. Your score = Chips × Mult. Higher hands score more. Jokers add extra Mult or Chips. The key to winning is building a joker setup that multiplies your score exponentially.

### Core Loop

1. **Play a Blind:** Play poker hands to reach the target score. You have 4 hands and 3 discards per round.
2. **Visit the Shop:** Spend money earned on jokers, card packs, tarot cards, and vouchers.
3. **Choose your next Blind:** Skip optional Small Blinds for money, or take the boss Blind for bonus rewards.
4. **Repeat for 8 Antes.**

### Beginner Tips

- **Focus on Mult, not Chips.** Flat Mult from jokers scales much better in the late game.
- **Don't hoard money.** Spend it on jokers that immediately improve your scoring.
- **Discard bad hands.** Quality over quantity.
- **Watch for synergies.** Jokers that work together are more powerful than single strong jokers.
- **Save your discards for boss Blinds.**
