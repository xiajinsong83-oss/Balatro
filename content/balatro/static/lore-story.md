Balatro is a game shrouded in mystery. While the gameplay takes center stage, the world has hidden depth.

### The Setting

You are a nameless poker player in a dark, underground casino known as "the House." The rules are simple: beat the Antes, earn your winnings, and climb to the top. But something about this place is off. The cards seem alive. The jokers have personalities. The Blinds have bosses with strange powers.

### The Jokers

Jokers are more than just scoring multipliers. Each joker has its own character, its own effect on the game, and its own place in the world. From the simple "Joker" to the legendary "Blueprint," these cards are your companions, your weapons, and your keys to victory.

### The House

The House is never seen, but always present. It sets the stakes. It deals the cards. It watches. The boss Blinds are its enforcers — each with a special rule that makes your run harder.

### Spoiler Warning

The deeper lore of Balatro is best discovered firsthand. We've kept this page spoiler-free. Focus on unlocking all decks, beating all stake levels, and experimenting with spectral cards.
